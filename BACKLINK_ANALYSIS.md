## 4. Recommendations for Link Building

To improve search engine visibility, a proactive and legitimate link-building strategy is essential. The focus must be on acquiring high-quality, relevant, and "DoFollow" links.

### Strategy 1: Local Citations and Directories

*   **Action**: Ensure your business is listed accurately on prominent Kenyan business directories, local review sites, and Google My Business.
*   **Benefit**: These provide foundational local SEO signals and legitimate backlinks.

### Strategy 2: Partnerships and Sponsorships

*   **Action**: Partner with local event planners, wedding venues, or charities in Nairobi. Offer to provide flowers in exchange for a mention and a link on their website's "Partners" or "Vendors" page.
*   **Benefit**: These links are highly relevant and often come from established local domains.

### Strategy 3: Content Marketing and Outreach

*   **Action**: Create valuable content on your website, such as a guide to "Seasonal Flowers in Kenya" or "How to Care for Fresh Cut Roses." Reach out to local lifestyle bloggers, wedding magazines, or news outlets and pitch your content as a resource.
*   **Benefit**: Earning links from authoritative content creators significantly boosts your site's Domain Authority.

### Strategy 4: Disavow Spam Links (Optional but Recommended)

*   **Action**: Although the current spam links are NoFollow, it is good practice to monitor your backlink profile. If you notice an influx of toxic "DoFollow" links from similar spam networks, you should use Google's Disavow Tool to tell Google to ignore them, protecting your site from potential penalties.
