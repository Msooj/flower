# Google Search Console Verification Instructions

## To verify your website with Google Search Console:

1. **Go to Google Search Console**: https://search.google.com/search-console/

2. **Add Property**: 
   - Select "URL prefix"
   - Enter: https://www.flowerlifestyle.co.ke/
   - Click "Continue"

3. **Verify Ownership**:
   - Choose "HTML file" method
   - Download the verification file
   - Upload it to your public folder
   - OR choose "DNS record" method for easier verification

4. **Submit Sitemap**:
   - After verification, go to "Sitemaps"
   - Submit: https://www.flowerlifestyle.co.ke/sitemap.xml
