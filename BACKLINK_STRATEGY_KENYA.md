## 3. Backlinks you can earn (ethical)

### A. Partners & suppliers
- Flower farms, event planners, hotels, cake shops → ask for a “Preferred florist” link to your homepage or `/florist-kenya`.

### B. Press & community
- Local blogs: Nairobi lifestyle, Kenyan weddings, “best gifts in Kenya” listicles — pitch: *“Same-day florist at City Market”* with link.
- Sponsor a small community event; ask organizers to list sponsors with your URL.

## 7. What to avoid

- Buying “1000 backlinks” on Fiverr
- Fake Google reviews
- Keyword stuffing on pages
- Duplicate content copied from other florists
- Fake `aggregateRating` in schema without real reviews

---

## 8. Measuring success

Track monthly:

- Google Search Console: clicks, impressions, average position for “florist kenya” / “flower delivery nairobi”
- GA4: sessions, `/flowers` → cart → checkout
- Orders via WhatsApp (ask “How did you find us?”)
- Google Business Profile: calls, direction requests, reviews

**Goal:** More branded searches (“Flower Lifestyle”), top-10 for long-tail phrases first, then broader terms as authority grows.
